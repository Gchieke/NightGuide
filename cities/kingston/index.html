Jack's Hill · $ · Kingston

dubroots reggaereggae

Legendary open-air Sunday session on Skyline Drive overlooking the city — 100% undiluted roots reggae and dub played on the Rockers Sound Station at host Gabre Selassie's home.

Hours
Sundays only, approx. 6pm–1am (gates from 5pm)
DJs & nights
  • Gabre Selassie & Rockers Sound Station (resident selector)

  • Weekly Sunday roots & dub session since 2002
Dress code
Casual — relaxed hilltop vibes
Entry
Contribution at the gate; food and drinks on sale
Prices
Contribution JMD 2,000 (half price for UWI/UTech students with ID)
Age policy
18+
