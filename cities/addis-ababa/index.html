Gazlight (Gaslight) at Sheraton Addis

Taitu Street, inside Sheraton Addis (near Meskel Square) · $$$ · Addis Ababa

afrobeatsedmhousehip hop

Addis Ababa's flagship hotel nightclub, relaunched as Gazlight in 2026 — an upscale, Western-style disco inside the Sheraton with resident DJs, themed party nights and a well-dressed crowd.

Hours
Opens early evening (happy hour from ~5:30 PM); headline Thu and Sat nights run until the early morning hours
DJs & nights
  • Thursdays @ Gazlight with resident DJs Papa, Klutch & Foxx
  • All Out Saturdays party nights
  • International guest DJ tours (e.g. DJ Consequence, Aug 2026)
Dress code
Strict: elegant, stylish and sophisticated — no jeans or trainers
Entry
No entrance fee; drinks are premium hotel-priced
Prices
No cover charge; premium drink prices in ETB
Age policy
18+ (19+ on some event nights per venue posts)

Fendika@Hyatt (Fendika Cultural Center)

Meskel Square, inside Hyatt Regency Addis Ababa · $ · Addis Ababa

azmariethio-jazzhabeshatraditional

Melaku Belay's legendary azmari bet, relocated into the Hyatt Regency after its Kazanchis home was demolished in 2024 — azmari singers improvise verses over masinko and kebero, with eskista dancing and Ethio-jazz sessions.

Hours
Select evenings, music from around 9:30 PM; no fixed published schedule — check @fendika_live socials or the hotel on the day
DJs & nights
  • Azmari nights with masinko, kebero and eskista dancers
  • Intimate jazz sessions on select evenings (Mon/Wed/Fri per tour listings)
  • Melaku Belay's Ethiocolor and guest traditional acts
Dress code
Casual; come as you are
Entry
Small cover charge; tip the azmari when they sing for you
Prices
Entry reported around 100–250 ETB; confirm on the day
Age policy
18+

African Jazz Village

Meskel Square area, inside Ghion Hotel · $$ · Addis Ababa

ethio-jazzjazzhabesha

Ethio-jazz pioneer Mulatu Astatke's club in the Ghion Hotel grounds — live bands play Ethio-jazz in a relaxed garden setting several nights a week; the city's essential jazz pilgrimage.

Hours
Show nights Mon, Thu and Fri; doors 7 PM, band from 9 PM (no live jazz Sat–Sun)
DJs & nights
  • Asli Band's long-running Thursday residency
  • Live Ethio-jazz bands on Mon, Thu and Fri nights
  • Guest legend nights (e.g. Admas Band)
Dress code
Smart casual
Entry
Cover charge at the door
Prices
Entry listed around 300 ETB (one listing also shows 'price on request' — take cash and confirm at the door)
Age policy
18+

Yod Abyssinia

Bole Medhanialem · $$ · Addis Ababa

traditionalhabeshafolklive music

Addis Ababa's best-known traditional cultural restaurant — a huge hall where singers and dozens of dancers perform music and dance from across Ethiopia's regions on stage while you eat; a nightly cultural showcase rather than a club.

Hours
Open daily 6 AM–11 PM; the stage show runs in the evening
DJs & nights
  • Nightly traditional music and dance stage show, roughly 7:30–10:30 PM
  • Regional dance troupes and live singers
  • Traditional coffee ceremony
Dress code
Casual
Entry
No separate cover; you pay for food and drink — reservations recommended
Prices
No entry fee; moderate ETB menu prices
Age policy
All ages (family-friendly dinner show)

habeshalive musicafrobeats

Club Illusion

Lideta, under Ambassador Theatre, Ras Desta Damtew Street · $$$ · Addis Ababa

habeshaafrobeatship hopdance

Addis Ababa's most raucous after-hours club, in the basement of the Ambassador Cinema — a packed, VIP-leaning dancefloor that fills late and is famous for dancing until dawn on weekends.

Hours
Late-night hours, busiest Thu–Sat until around 5 AM
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJ dance nights
  • Packed Friday and Saturday party nights
  • After-hours sessions running to around 5 AM
Dress code
Dress to impress; the door can be selective on busy nights
Entry
Cover charge; varies by night
Prices
Listed as an expensive venue; cover and drinks in ETB vary by night
Age policy
18+
