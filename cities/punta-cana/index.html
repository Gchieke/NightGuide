Coco Bongo Punta Cana

Downtown Punta Cana (Las Mercedes) · $$$ · Punta Cana

top 40latinpopdance

Punta Cana's most famous night out — a Vegas-style show-club mixing DJ sets with tribute acts, acrobats and aerialists rather than a conventional dance club.

Hours
Nightly, doors around 10:30 PM, show runs to about 3 AM
DJs & nights
  • Tribute shows: Michael Jackson, Queen, Madonna
  • Acrobat and aerialist acts between DJ sets
  • Confetti and balloon-drop party moments all night
Dress code
Smart casual; no strict dress code
Entry
Ticketed packages only, most include open bar and hotel transfer
Prices
Open-bar packages roughly USD 80–200 (regular open bar around USD 85); venues here price in USD
Age policy
18+

Imagine Punta Cana (The Cave)

Friusa, between Bavaro and Downtown · $$ · Punta Cana

reggaetonlatinhouseedm

A multi-level nightclub built inside a natural limestone cave system, with different rooms playing different music under the stalactites.

Hours
Themed nights Thu–Sat, approx. 11 PM–4 AM (open bar typically until ~3 AM)
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs across multiple cave levels
  • Reggaeton and Latin room
  • Fire-breathing and flair-bartending shows
Dress code
Smart casual; no flip-flops
Entry
Ticket packages, usually including open bar and hotel pickup
Prices
Around USD 50–75 including open bar
Age policy
18+

Oro Nightclub

Hard Rock Hotel & Casino, Arena Gorda corridor · $$$ · Punta Cana

edmhousetop 40

The area's luxury superclub — a 14,000+ sq ft two-level room inside the Hard Rock with a Funktion One sound system, open to non-guests.

Hours
Wed–Sat, approx. 11 PM–4 AM
DJs & nights
  • International EDM headliners and guest DJs
  • Resident DJ nights across two levels
  • Themed party and tour-DJ events
Dress code
Upscale; dress to impress, no beachwear
Entry
Cover charge; ladies enter free on many club nights
Prices
Cover varies by night and event
Age policy
18+
