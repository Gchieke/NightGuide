The 9 clubs

Berghain

Friedrichshain (Am Wriezener Bahnhof) · $$ · Berlin

technohouseambient

The world's most famous techno club — a former power plant with 18m ceilings, four Funktion-One towers, and a door policy as legendary as the sound.

Hours
Klubnacht: Sat 11:59pm–Mon ~midday (runs continuously)
DJs & nights
  • Residents: Ben Klock, Marcel Dettmann
  • Weekend Klubnacht marathon
  • Panorama Bar (house: Steffi, Prosumer) upstairs
Dress code
Understated all-black; no flashy, touristy or sportswear looks. Famously the strictest door in the city
Entry
Small groups or pairs, quiet in the queue; Sunday morning better odds
Prices
€20–€25 entry
Age policy
18+

Tresor

Mitte (Köpenicker Straße 70) · $ · Berlin

technohouseelectro

The underground institution — a smoke-filled, red-lit former heating plant where Berlin techno was born.

Hours
Wed/Fri/Sat, ~11:45pm–6am
DJs & nights
  • Birthplace of Berlin techno (est. 1991)
  • Hosted Detroit legends Jeff Mills and Robert Hood
  • Three interlinked floors plus the vault basement
Dress code
Casual and dark; no sportswear. Door selective but easier than Berghain
Entry
Door selective but easier than Berghain
Prices
€12–€18 entry
Age policy
18+

Sisyphos

Lichtenberg (Hauptstraße 15) · $$ · Berlin

technohouseminimal

A festival disguised as a club — named TimeOut's best nightclub in Europe, with a permanent-summer-festival feel and an anything-goes crowd.

Hours
Extended weekends Fri–Mon (routinely Friday night to Monday morning)
DJs & nights
  • Weekend-long marathon parties
  • Huge outdoor garden with daytime house sets
  • Five indoor stages
Dress code
No pretentious dress code — relaxed and bohemian. Strict no-photos policy
Entry
Strict no-photos policy; door can be selective
Prices
€18–€25 entry
Age policy
18+

Salon zur Wilden Renate

Friedrichshain (Alt-Stralau 70) · $$ · Berlin

housetechnodiscoelectro

A giant house party in a maze-like old residential building — saved from closure by a 10-year lease in 2025, renovated and fully back in 2026.

Thu 11pm–8am, Fri 11pm–9am, Sat 11pm–10am

Entry
CARD PAYMENT ONLY (no cash)
Prices
€15–€20 entry
