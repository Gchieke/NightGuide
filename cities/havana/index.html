The 10 clubs

La Zorra y el Cuervo

Vedado (La Rampa, Calle 23) · $$ · Havana

jazzlatin jazzlive music

Havana's most respected jazz club — a tiny basement cellar entered through a red British phone box, where top Cuban jazz musicians play serious late sets to a listening crowd of about sixty.

Hours
Nightly from ~10:00 PM to ~2:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Nightly live jazz sets from around 10:00 PM
  • Rotating Cuban jazz combos and guest soloists
  • Jazz Plaza festival stage in January
Dress code
Casual smart; no strict code
Entry
Walk-ins only, no reservations — queue forms from about 9:30 PM
Prices
Around $10 USD for visitors and usually includes two drinks; prices in CUP for locals vary with exchange rates
Age policy
18+

Casa de la Música Miramar

Miramar · $$ · Havana

salsatimbasonlive music

The city's temple of live salsa and timba since the mid-1990s — a big hall with table service where the major Cuban dance orchestras play to a crowd that actually dances.

Hours
Daily, two sessions ~5:00–9:00 PM and ~11:00 PM–3:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Nightly headline salsa/timba orchestras
  • Matinee session 5:00–9:00 PM popular with local dancers
  • Late session from ~11:00 PM to 3:00 AM
Dress code
Smart casual
Entry
Pay at the door; tables can be held — check the night's billing the same day
Prices
About $10–25 USD depending on the band; visitors generally pay in USD/EUR cash
Age policy
18+

Cabaret Tropicana

Marianao · $$$ · Havana

cabaretlive musiclatinshow

The legendary open-air cabaret running since 1939 — a huge feathered revue with a live orchestra and 200+ performers under the trees, Havana's most famous night out.

Hours
Doors ~8:30–9:00 PM, show ~9:30–10:00 PM, closes ~1:00 AM (most nights; some sources say Wed–Sun)
DJs & nights
  • Nightly 'Paradise Under the Stars' revue
  • Live big-band orchestra
  • Dancing after the show
Dress code
Smart elegant — no shorts, T-shirts or sportswear
Entry
Ticketed by seat tier; book ahead in peak season, box office sells same-day in low season
Prices
Tourist ticket pricing in USD: roughly $85–150 per person by seat tier, more with dinner; rum included in most tickets
Age policy
18+

El Gato Tuerto

Vedado (Calle O, near Hotel Nacional) · $ · Havana

bolerofilintrovalive music

The surviving home of filin and bolero since 1960 — an intimate concert café where veteran singers perform slow Cuban song up close; nobody dances, everybody listens.

Hours
Nightly from ~10:00 PM to ~2:30 AM
DJs & nights
  • Two live concerts nightly
  • Billed concerts Fri–Sat from ~11:30 PM
  • Bolero and filin singers with house musicians
Dress code
Casual smart
Entry
Walk-in; arrive soon after opening to secure a table
Prices
Entry around $5 USD / €5 for visitors; moderate drink prices
Age policy
18+

Jazz Café

Vedado (Galerías Paseo, opposite Meliá Cohiba) · $$ · Havana

jazzlatin jazzlive music

A table-service jazz supper club — the comfortable alternative to the basement rooms, with dinner, reservations and one long late set from name Cuban jazz players.

Hours
Thu–Sun from ~9:00 PM, late set ~11:00 PM
DJs & nights
  • Late jazz set around 11:00 PM
  • Cuban jazz quartets and guest acts
  • Thu–Sun programming
Dress code
Smart casual
Entry
Reservations accepted; cover varies by night
Prices
Cover varies by act; visitors typically charged in USD/EUR
Age policy
18+

Submarino Amarillo

Vedado (Calle 17) · $ · Havana

rockpoplive music

The Beatles-themed Yellow Submarine cultural centre — Havana's rock refuge, where Cuban bands play rock and pop covers plus 'rockoteca' DJ nights to a young local crowd.

Hours
Tue–Sun 9:00 PM–2:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Live rock cover bands
  • Rockoteca DJ nights
  • Sports-bar screenings on match nights
Dress code
Casual
Entry
Small cover at the door
Prices
Cover about $5 USD for visitors; cheap local drinks pricing in CUP
Age policy
18+

Café Teatro Bertolt Brecht

Vedado (Calle 13) · $ · Havana

jazzfusiontimbalive music

A state cultural centre with the best Wednesday night in Havana — Roberto Carcassés' Interactivo fuses jazz, funk and timba in a theatre-bar setting that draws musicians and students.

Hours
Event nights; concerts typically from ~9:00 PM, Interactivo Wednesdays late
DJs & nights
  • Interactivo jazz-fusion sessions Wednesdays ~11:30 PM
  • Rotating concert programme
  • Theatre and comedy nights
Dress code
Casual
Entry
Ticket/cover at the door, varies by event
Prices
Cover varies by event; generally a few USD for visitors, CUP pricing for locals
Age policy
18+

Salón Rosado Benny Moré de La Tropical

Marianao (Calle 41 y 44) · $ · Havana

timbasalsalive music

An enormous open-air dance hall where Havana itself — not the tourist circuit — goes to hear the big timba orchestras, mostly on Saturday nights. Raw, loud and local.

Hours
Mainly Saturday nights; occasional other sessions — check locally
DJs & nights
  • Saturday-night timba/salsa orchestras
  • Occasional weekday concerts
  • Daytime matinees some Sundays
Dress code
Casual
Entry
Pay at the gate; programming is sporadic — confirm locally the same week
Prices
Local pricing in CUP, reported around 100–200 CUP entry — only a few USD even at tourist exchange rates
Age policy
18+

Cabaret Parisien

Vedado (Hotel Nacional de Cuba) · $$$ · Havana

cabaretlive musiclatinshow

The Nacional's historic cabaret, staging the colourful 'Habana de Fiesta' / 'Cubano Cubano' revue with live band and dancers, followed by audience dancing — Tropicana glamour at half the price.

Hours
9:00 PM–2:00 AM, most nights (closed Wednesdays per hotel); show at 10:00 PM
DJs & nights
  • Nightly cabaret revue at 10:00 PM
  • Live orchestra and dance troupe
  • Dancing after the show
Dress code
Smart elegant — dress code enforced
Entry
Ticketed; show-only or dinner-and-show packages, bookable via the hotel
Prices
Show-only about $32–45 USD at the hotel (includes 2 drinks); dinner-and-show package listed at $75 USD
Age policy
18+

Habana Café

Vedado (Hotel Meliá Cohiba, Paseo) · $$ · Havana

sontraditional cubanlive musiccabaret

A 1950s-themed nightclub-cabaret in the Meliá Cohiba with vintage cars as decor, staging traditional Cuban music shows — including Buena Vista-generation artists — with dinner and dancing.

Hours
Nightly from ~9:00 PM to ~1:00–2:30 AM
DJs & nights
  • Nightly traditional Cuban music show from ~9:00 PM
  • Buena Vista-style orchestras on hotel show nights
  • Dancing between sets
Dress code
Smart casual — hotel dress code applies
Entry
Cover/minimum spend at the door; often packaged as dinner-and-show by the hotel
Prices
Hotel cabaret pricing in USD; cover varies by act and package — confirm with the hotel
Age policy
18+
