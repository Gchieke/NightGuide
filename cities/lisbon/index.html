Lux Frágil

Santa Apolónia docklands · $$$ · Lisbon

housetechnodisco

Portugal's most famous club — a Tagus-side warehouse with two dance floors, big-name guests and a rooftop terrace made for sunrise.

Hours
Thu–Sat 12am–6am (bar from 10pm)
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJ Rui Vargas
  • International house and techno guests
  • EXOTIKA queer party residency
Dress code
Smart and fashionable — strict door; no sportswear
Entry
Famously selective door — entry never guaranteed; arrive in small groups
Prices
€15–25 entry
Age policy
18+
