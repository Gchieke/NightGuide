Echostage

Langdon, Northeast DC · $$ · Washington DC

edmhousetrancebass music

DC's mega-club: a 30,000 sq ft, 3,000-capacity warehouse room near Ivy City run by Club Glow/Insomniac, repeatedly ranked in DJ Mag's world Top 10 (#1 in 2021) with festival-grade sound and LED production.

Hours
Event nights, mostly Fri–Sat, doors ~9–10 PM until ~3–4 AM
DJs & nights
  • Club Glow headline nights with arena-tier touring EDM DJs
  • Project GLOW festival team events
  • Trance, bass, house and big-room bookings weekly
Dress code
Casual — no formal dress code unless an event states otherwise
Entry
Ticketed events; advance tickets online or at the box office on the night
Prices
Big-name DJ nights typically ticketed around $30–$80+ depending on the headliner
Age policy
18+ (most club nights; some events 21+)

Flash

Shaw (Florida Ave NW) · $$ · Washington DC

housetechno

DC's serious dance-music room since 2013: a three-level club on Florida Ave with an acoustically treated main room built around a custom Funktion-One system, plus a ground-floor bar and a rooftop.

Hours
Club nights Fri–Sat from ~10 PM–3 AM; bar and rooftop open earlier in the evening
DJs & nights
  • International house and techno headliners Fri–Sat
  • Rooftop sessions in season
  • Resident and local DJ nights on the bar level
Dress code
Casual
Entry
Cover or advance tickets at the door
Prices
Typically $10–$40 depending on the lineup; resident nights from around $10
Age policy
21+

housetechnoafrobeats

A 600-capacity warehouse-style event space in Ivy City hosting regular club programming — touring house and techno DJs, day parties and themed takeovers — rather than operating as a nightly walk-in club.

Hours
Event-based: most club nights 10 PM–3 AM, occasional Sunday day parties from ~3 PM
DJs & nights
  • Nu Androids presents touring house/techno nights
  • Church on Sunday day-party series
  • Themed takeovers such as Afro Rave Weekend
Dress code
Casual; varies by event
Entry
Advance tickets via event platforms; door sales when available
Prices
Ticket prices vary by event
Age policy
21+

Ultrabar

Downtown / Penn Quarter (F St NW) · $$ · Washington DC

hip hoptop 40latinhouse

DC's longest-running multi-floor club, in a former bank at 911 F St NW: each level plays a different format — Top 40/hip hop on the main floor, Latin in the Bedroom, house in the basement Vault lounge and the Penthouse.

Hours
Fri–Sat 10 PM–3 AM
DJs & nights
  • Main floor: Top 40 and hip hop
  • Bedroom level: Latin rhythms
  • Vault basement and Penthouse: house and international mashups
Dress code
Strictly enforced — dress to impress; no baggy jeans, athletic wear, sneakers, hats or work boots
Entry
Cover at the door; guest-list and free-admission promos run on the venue's site
Prices
Cover varies by night, typically in the $10–$30 range
Age policy
21+

Zebbie's Garden

Dupont Circle (Connecticut Ave NW) · $$$ · Washington DC

hip hopr&bafrobeatshouse

An upscale rooftop club on the third floor of the historic Mayflower building — a Prohibition-era speakeasy reimagined as an indoor garden with a retractable glass roof, neon and cherry-blossom décor, hookah and bottle service.

Hours
Thu 10 PM–2 AM; Fri–Sat 10 PM–3 AM
DJs & nights
  • Garden Thursdays rooftop party (open bar early, free entry before midnight)
  • Weekend DJs spinning hip hop, R&B, afrobeats and house
  • Late-night dance sets under the retractable roof
Dress code
Polished and fashion-forward; club attire required — no athletic wear or sandals/flats
Entry
Typically no cover; tables by reservation with minimum spend
Prices
No cover most nights; bottle-service tables from around $500 minimum
Age policy
21+

The Mayflower Club

Dupont Circle (Connecticut Ave NW) · $$$ · Washington DC

latinreggaetondembowtop 40

A multi-level lounge and nightclub in a historic speakeasy building (sharing the address with Zebbie's Garden) that runs DC's biggest weekend Latin parties, alongside a Mediterranean dinner service earlier in the evening.

Hours
Fri–Sat club nights 10 PM–3 AM (venue opens from ~6 PM for dinner and drinks); Sun 4–9:30 PM
DJs & nights
  • Seduction Saturdays — the city's flagship Latin party, ladies' open bar until midnight
  • Friday Latin nights: reggaeton, dembow and Top 40
  • Latin holiday and themed parties
Dress code
Upscale / chic
Entry
Free-entry promotions on many nights via RSVP platforms; bottle service available
Prices
Free entry on many promo nights; cover varies by event
Age policy
21+
