The 10 clubs

Savaya Bali

Uluwatu · $$$ · Bali

houseedmtech house

Clifftop day-to-night club perched 100 metres above the Indian Ocean in Uluwatu — formerly Omnia — famous for its illuminated Cube and international DJ line-ups.

Hours
Mon–Thu 1pm–10pm; Fri & Sun 1pm–close; Sat 3pm–close
DJs & nights
  • International headliners on the 2026 calendar incl. Snakehips and Nico de Andrea
  • Sunset sets before headline shows
  • Resident DJs opening and closing event nights
Dress code
Smart casual — no beer-branded singlets, cut-offs or sportswear
Entry
Ticketed entry only, sold via its official / Megatix channels; general admission is listed as strictly 21+ with photo ID
Prices
General admission Rp500,000–Rp850,000 depending on the event (sunset tickets from Rp500,000); VIP tables and daybeds extra
Age policy
18+

Finns Beach Club

Canggu · $$ · Bali

houseedmcommercial dance

Massive beachfront club on Berawa Beach in Canggu with multiple pools, swim-up bars and restaurants, billed as the world's best beach club and busiest at sunset.

Hours
Daily 11am–12am (VIP beach club from 10am)
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs daily, with live vocalists from around 4pm
  • Daily sunset party sets
  • International headliners for big events, incl. Duke Dumont, MK and PNAU for New Year's Eve
Dress code
Beach casual chic — no alcohol-branded, gang or matching group outfits; door policy is strictly enforced
Entry
Free entry with no minimum spend for bar and restaurant areas; daybeds are booked for a fee credited to food and drink
Prices
Free entry; daybed booking fees from around Rp650,000 for a single bed, fully credited to food and drink
Age policy
18+

Atlas Beach Club

Canggu · $$ · Bali

edmhousecommercial dance

Sprawling Berawa beach club claiming to be the world's largest beach club, with a huge pool, daybeds and the Atlas Super Club nightclub on the same site.

Hours
Daily 12pm–12am; Atlas Super Club 10pm–4am
DJs & nights
  • Daily resident DJs and live performances
  • Atlas Super Club themed nights incl. Afro, ladies and techno nights
  • International guest DJs for festival-style events
Dress code
Beach attire by day, smart casual in the evening; no alcohol-branded clothing in the Super Club
Entry
Paid entry (unlike most Bali beach clubs); the on-site Super Club runs separately at night, 10pm–4am, with its own first-drink charge
Prices
Entry from around Rp100,000–Rp200,000, day passes around Rp300,000 on some channels; daybeds from Rp1,000,000 minimum spend
Age policy
18+

ShiShi Nightclub & Izakaya Lounge

Petitenget · $$ · Bali

edmhouser&bhip-hop

Three-floor Seminyak venue on Jalan Petitenget combining a Japanese izakaya restaurant downstairs with R&B and main-room club floors above, partying until 4am.

Hours
Daily 6pm–4am (izakaya from 5–6pm, club floors from 9–10pm)
DJs & nights
  • Wednesday Ladies Night with free entry and free-flow drinks early
  • Guest DJs every Friday and Saturday
  • Resident DJs — main room EDM / house, separate R&B and hip-hop room
Dress code
Upscale fashionable / smart casual — no singlets, hats, sandals, beachwear or sportswear
Entry
First-drink-charge system; the venue is 21+ with valid ID, and dinner guests get complimentary club access
Prices
Men: first-drink charge Rp250,000; ladies: Rp150,000, free Sun–Thu; prices vary for special events
Age policy
18+

Vault Bali

Canggu · $$ · Bali

housetechnohip-hopafrobeats

Underground, Berlin-style bunker club beneath a complex on Jalan Pantai Berawa in Canggu, built around a Void Acoustics sound system and a music-first crowd.

Hours
10pm–4am on club nights (usually Mon–Wed & Fri–Sat; check listings)
DJs & nights
  • Cheddar Wednesdays — Afrobeats to Amapiano
  • Friday house and techno nights
  • Saturday hip-hop to R&B, Monday R&B to future beats
Dress code
Stylish casual / club attire — no flip-flops
Entry
Paid general admission on the door; guest-list and VIP bottle-service options available
Prices
General admission around Rp150,000–Rp200,000, higher for international guest events
Age policy
18+

La Favela Bali

Seminyak · $$ · Bali

commercial dancehip-hopreggaeton

Jungle- and vintage-themed multi-level restaurant-turned-nightclub on Seminyak's Eat Street, famous for its maze of decorated rooms and packed late-night dance floors.

Hours
Daily 7pm–3am, Fri–Sat until 4am (dinner from 7pm, party from around 11pm)
DJs & nights
  • Guest and resident DJs from around 11pm nightly
  • Commercial open-format sets
  • Busiest Thu–Sat weekend nights
Dress code
Smart casual — no flip-flops or singlets
Entry
Cover charge after 10pm on busy nights; arrive before 11pm to beat the queue
Prices
Ladies free; men around Rp200,000–Rp250,000 after 10pm; table minimum spends Rp4,000,000+
Age policy
18+

Motel Mexicola

Seminyak · $$ · Bali

latincommercial danceparty classics

Colourful 1960s-Acapulco-style Mexican cantina and tequileria in Petitenget, Seminyak, that turns from tacos and margaritas into a dancing-on-tables fiesta after dark.

Hours
Daily 11am–1am, Fri–Sat until 1:30am
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs and live fiesta entertainment nightly
  • Latin party sets
  • Late-night party sessions from around 10pm
Dress code
Casual to smart casual — no strict dress code
Entry
Free entry on regular nights, reservations recommended; special events such as New Year's Eve are ticketed
Prices
Free entry on regular nights — pay only for food and drink; ticketed prices apply for special fiesta events
Age policy
18+

Potato Head Beach Club

Petitenget · $$$ · Bali

housediscotechnobalearic

Iconic Seminyak beach club at Desa Potato Head behind a facade of reclaimed teak shutters, known for sunsets, sustainability and its DJ Harvey-designed Klymax Discotheque.

Hours
Daily 11am–11pm (beach club); Klymax Discotheque on club nights late
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJ Dita and the Potato Head resident roster
  • Klymax Discotheque nights curated with DJ Harvey
  • International takeovers and guests, incl. Dekmantel events
Dress code
Casual chic — swimwear is fine around the pool but not inside the restaurants
Entry
Free entry before 4:30pm, then a redeemable sunset cover; daybeds and Klymax event nights are booked separately
Prices
Free before 4:30pm; Rp250,000 cover 4:30–7pm, fully redeemable for food and drink; lounge seating minimum spend from Rp500,000, beach beds from Rp3,000,000
Age policy
18+

White Rock Beach Club

Uluwatu · $$ · Bali

housecommercial dancesunset

Large beachfront club on Melasti Beach in Ungasan, below the Uluwatu cliffs, with lagoon and rock pools, cabanas and party suites built for sunset and event days.

Hours
Daily 10am–10pm
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs daily from 12:30pm
  • Saturday and Sunday foam parties from 3:15pm
  • Sunset parties with guest DJs, saxophone and percussion on special days
Dress code
Swimwear and beachwear for pool areas; dry, appropriate attire required in the restaurant — no wet swimwear inside
Entry
Free entry except special events and New Year's Eve; a separate small Melasti Beach gate fee is collected by the local village
Prices
Free entry (Melasti Beach gate around Rp10,000–Rp15,000); daybed and sofa minimum spend from around Rp1,000,000, credited to food and drink
Age policy
18+

Single Fin

Uluwatu · $ · Bali

houselive musicparty classics

Legendary clifftop surf bar above Suluban Beach overlooking the Uluwatu break, home to the famous Sunday Session that draws surfers, locals and travellers until late.

Hours
Daily 8am–10pm; Wed & Sun until 2am
DJs & nights
  • Legendary Sunday Sessions with live music and DJs
  • Wednesday late-night parties until 2am
  • Sunset DJ sets and live bands
Dress code
Casual surf / beach casual — no strict dress code
Entry
Free entry; walk-in, arrive early on Sundays to get a spot
Prices
Free entry — pay only for food and drink, at surf-bar prices well below the big beach clubs
Age policy
18+
