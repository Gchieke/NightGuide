VICE Nightclub

Cipriani Boulevard, Newtown · $$ · Port of Spain

socadancehallhip hopedm

Port of Spain's flagship big-room club, which took over the legendary 51 Degrees site on Cipriani Boulevard. Multi-room layout hosting major soca concerts, Carnival-season events and themed party nights.

Hours
Thu–Sat 10:00 PM–5:00 AM; Sun reggae nights 9:00 PM–2:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Slam 100.5FM Soca Power concert series
  • Sunday 'Reggae in the Heart of the City' nights
  • Rotating local soca and dancehall DJs
Dress code
Smart and stylish — dress to impress
Entry
Cover charge at the door; advance tickets for headline concert nights
Prices
Reggae Sundays: ladies free before 11 PM, then TT$60; men TT$100. Other nights vary by event
Age policy
18+

Zen Nightclub

Keate Street, Downtown · $$$ · Port of Spain

edmhousedancehallsoca

Trinidad's most famous dance-music institution, in a converted art-deco cinema on Keate Street. Recently renovated and reopened, it now operates mainly as a special-events venue with big production nights.

Hours
Event nights only, typically 10:00 PM–4:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • 'One Night in Zen' headline DJ events

  • Carnival fete takeovers
Dress code
Smart dress code strictly enforced
Entry
