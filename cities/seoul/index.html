Cakeshop

Itaewon · $$ · Seoul

housetechnouk garagebasship-hop

Long-running Itaewon basement club (est. 2012) that anchors Seoul's underground, booking international house, techno and bass names alongside top local residents in a raw 200-capacity room with a terrace.

Hours
Thu–Sun 10pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Rotating international guest DJs
  • Deep bench of local resident DJs
  • Seoul underground crew takeovers
Dress code
Relaxed — sneakers, tees and streetwear are fine
Entry
Cover at the door; foreigners must show a physical passport (phone photos and foreign licences are not accepted)
Prices
₩15,000–₩25,000
Age policy
19+

Entry

Sinsa, Gangnam · $$$ · Seoul

edmbig roomhip-hop

Gangnam's big-room survivor by Sinsa Station — the former Club Race reborn as ACE, with separate EDM and hip-hop zones and a dress-up, table-service culture.

Hours
Tue–Sun 11pm–9am (closed Mon)
DJs & nights
  • Resident EDM DJs in the main zone
  • Hip-hop zone DJs
