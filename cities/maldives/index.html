Subsix (Niyama Private Islands Maldives)

Niyama Private Islands, Dhaalu Atoll · $$$ · Maldives

houseedmtop 40

The world's first underwater nightclub, six metres below the lagoon and reached by speedboat from the resort; by day it serves underwater Nikkei dining, and on party nights the reef-facing room becomes a glowing dancefloor. Access is for Niyama resort guests only.

Hours
Glow Parties on selected nights, approx. 9:00 PM – 12:00 AM; underwater dining by day
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJ at the underwater Glow Parties
  • Glow Party nights, typically Wednesdays and Saturdays
  • International guest DJs during the festive season
Dress code
Smart resort wear; white or neon glows best under the UV party lighting
Entry
Niyama resort guests only; Glow Party entry free for guests (18+)
Prices
Glow Party entry free for resort guests; underwater set lunches around USD 120–250 per person
Age policy
18+

15Below (W Maldives)

Fesdu Island, North Ari Atoll · $$$ · Maldives

housedancetop 40

The Maldives' only underground nightclub, buried beneath the beach at W Maldives and carried through the resort's full March 2025 relaunch, hosting international guest DJs. Access is for W Maldives guests only.

Hours
Club nights on selected evenings, late night (weekly schedule set by the resort)
DJs & nights
  • International guest DJs
  • Resident DJs on club nights
  • Sets curated under W Hotels' music programme
Dress code
Smart, stylish club wear; no swimwear
Entry
W Maldives resort guests only
Prices
Pricing set by the resort; drinks-led, no published cover charge
Age policy
18+

Beach Club (Seaside Finolhu)

Finolhu, Baa Atoll · $$$ · Maldives

housediscoretro

Retro-chic beach club and pool deck at Seaside Finolhu — barefoot dancing to resident and guest DJs until 1 AM, an upper shisha deck, and the famous White Party out on the 1.8 km sandbank. Access is for Finolhu resort guests only.

Hours
Daily 10:00 AM – 1:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJ with daily beach-club sets
  • International guest DJs on weekend residencies
  • Weekly White Party with DJ on the sandbank
Dress code
Barefoot beach-chic; all white for the White Party
Entry
Seaside Finolhu resort guests only
Prices
Pricing set by the resort; drinks by the glass, many party nights included for in-house guests
Age policy
18+

Hard Rock Hotel Maldives

Emboodhoo Lagoon, South Malé Atoll · $$$ · Maldives

rockpoptop 40

Music-obsessed resort at CROSSROADS with a Friday DJ pool party (underwater speakers included), weekly DJ nights and nightly live music at the oceanfront Hard Rock Cafe. The Marina @ CROSSROADS venues also welcome day visitors from Malé; the hotel pool scene is for guests.

Hours
Pool Bar from 9:00 AM; Hard Rock Cafe 12:00 PM – 12:30 AM; Friday pool party 1:30 PM – 5:00 PM
DJs & nights
  • Resident Deviate Band live at Hard Rock Cafe
  • Weekly DJ night at The Elephant and The Butterfly
  • Friday DJ pool party at the Pool Bar
Dress code
Casual rock-and-roll; swimwear fine at daytime pool parties
Entry
Hotel guests; Hard Rock Cafe and The Marina @ CROSSROADS open to day visitors by ferry/speedboat from Malé
Prices
Day-visitor access to The Marina is free; food and drinks at international resort-bar prices
Age policy
18+

Kandu Bar (Niva Kurumba Maldives)

Vihamanaafushi, North Malé Atoll · $$ · Maldives

popjazztop 40

Open-air pavilion bar with a wide overwater deck at Niva Kurumba (formerly Kurumba), ten minutes by speedboat from Malé, running the country's most reliable nightly entertainment — live bands, DJ evenings and traditional Bodu Beru. Access is via the resort; guests only, with visits by arrangement.

Hours
Daily 10:00 AM – 1:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • DJ Paiday's weekly DJ evenings
  • Live bands most nights
  • Tuesday Bodu Beru traditional drum night
Dress code
Relaxed resort casual
Entry
Resort guests; day/evening visits possible by arrangement with the resort
Prices
Among the Maldives' more affordable resort bars; drinks by the glass, prices set by the resort
Age policy
18+
