Best nightclubs in Prague

🇨🇿 Prague, Czechia · 10 clubs · hours, DJs, dress codes, entry prices & rules

Planning a night out in Prague? The 10 clubs

Roxy

Old Town (Staré Město) · $$ · Prague

technohousedrum and bass

Prague's most respected club, a former 1920s cinema on Dlouhá street with serious bookings across techno, house and drum & bass.

Hours
Fri–Sat 11pm–5am, plus Free Mondays and event nights
DJs & nights
  • Climax — long-running resident party night
  • Free Mondays with local DJs
  • International techno, house and drum & bass guests
Dress code
Casual — come as you are
Entry
Tickets on the door or cheaper in advance via GoOut; long queues on headline nights
Prices
200–600 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

Karlovy Lázně

Old Town, by Charles Bridge · $$ · Prague

popedmhip-hopoldies

Central Europe's largest club — five floors of pop, EDM, hip-hop and oldies inside a 15th-century former spa, hugely popular with visitors.

Hours
Daily 9pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs on five themed floors
  • Main-floor EDM and chart sets
  • Hip-hop, oldies and chill-out floor DJs
Dress code
Smart casual; no sportswear
Entry
Pay on the door; very tourist-oriented, expect queues at weekends
Prices
300–500 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

Cross Club

Holešovice · $ · Prague

drum and basstechnodubreggae

A steampunk labyrinth of welded scrap metal and kinetic sculptures hosting drum & bass, techno, dub and live gigs across multiple stages.

Hours
Daily 6pm–5am (Fri–Sat until 7am); café from 2pm
DJs & nights
  • Drum & bass and dubstep nights
  • Reggae and dub sessions
  • Live gigs and techno parties on the main stage
Dress code
Casual / alternative
Entry
Free entry most weekdays; small cover on weekend party nights
Prices
Free–250 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

Chapeau Rouge

Old Town (Staré Město) · $ · Prague

housepoptechnodrum and bass

Three-floor Old Town institution — a pub at street level, a commercial dance floor, and a basement running techno and drum & bass until dawn.

Hours
Daily — bar from midday, club floors until 5am
DJs & nights
  • Commercial house and pop on the dance floor
  • Techno and drum & bass in the basement
Dress code
Casual; smarter at weekends
Entry
Free before 11pm; cover charge after
Prices
Free–200 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

Lucerna Music Bar

New Town, off Wenceslas Square · $ · Prague

popdiscorock

Art Nouveau ballroom inside Lucerna Palace, famous for its packed Friday and Saturday 80s & 90s video disco and live concerts the rest of the week.

Hours
Fri–Sat Video Party 10pm–3am; concerts other nights from 7pm
DJs & nights
  • DJ Jirka Neumann's 80s & 90s Video Party (Fri & Sat)
  • Live Czech and international bands on weeknights
Dress code
Casual to smart
Entry
Pay on arrival — cash at the door; the video party is strictly 18+
Prices
150–250 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

Duplex

Wenceslas Square, New Town · $$ · Prague

commercial houser&bpop

Multi-floor club on Wenceslas Square with a glass rooftop terrace overlooking the square and the National Museum.

Hours
Daily 10pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs playing commercial house and R&B
  • Themed party nights and rooftop sessions
Dress code
Smart casual
Entry
Tickets online or on the door; the rooftop fills early at weekends
Prices
200–400 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

edmbig-room househard dance

Hours
Wed–Sun 10pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • International EDM and hard-dance headliners

Dress code
Smart casual — no sportswear
Entry
Advance tickets recommended; VIP tables bookable via the website

200–800 Kč entry
Age policy

M1 Lounge

Old Town (Masná) · $$ · Prague

r&bhip-hophouse

Prague's best-known R&B and hip-hop club for over 20 years — an upscale lounge-club with cocktails, shisha and VIP bottle service.

Hours
Wed–Sun 10pm–4am (Fri–Sat until 6am)
DJs & nights
  • Freaky Fridays — weekly R&B and hip-hop night
  • Resident DJs incl. DJ Illegal
Dress code
Smart and casual — no sport clothes, no hats (house rule)
Entry
Guest list and table reservations via the website; around 300 Kč on the door for event nights
Prices
200–300 Kč entry
Age policy
18+

SaSaZu

Holešovice · $$$ · Prague

edmhip-hoppop

Large riverside event venue by the Prague Market hosting major international concerts, headline DJ nights and festival afterparties.

Hours
Event nights only — doors typically from 7pm
DJs & nights
  • International concert bookings (e.g. Trippie Redd, Portugal. The Man)
  • Signal Festival afterparties
  • Occasional headline DJ events
Dress code
Casual to smart, event-dependent
Entry
Event nights only — advance tickets; no regular weekly club programme
Prices
600–1,500 Kč event tickets
Age policy
18+

Fuchs2

Štvanice Island, Holešovice · $ · Prague

technoelectroexperimental

Raw underground venue in a former island sports hall — Prague's home for cutting-edge techno and electro, with the Bike Jesus space next door.

Hours
Fri–Sat approx. 11pm–7am (event-based)
DJs & nights
  • Underground techno and electro line-ups
  • Queer-friendly party collectives
  • Live gigs at the adjoining Bike Jesus
Dress code
Casual / rave wear
Entry
Tickets via GoOut or Resident Advisor; event-based programme

150–300 Kč entry
