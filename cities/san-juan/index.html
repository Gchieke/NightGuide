Club Brava

Isla Verde · $$$ · San Juan

reggaetonlatinhip hophouse

Puerto Rico's marquee resort nightclub, inside the Fairmont El San Juan Hotel, with a main floor plus the upstairs Ultra Lounge and full bottle service.

Hours
Thu 10pm–4am; Fri–Sat 11pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Latin Billboard & reggaeton Saturdays
  • House & techno Fridays in Ultra Lounge
  • Guest DJ special events
Dress code
Strictly enforced upscale: no flip-flops, shorts, tank tops or athletic wear
Entry
Cover charge at the door; cheaper with advance RSVP
Prices
Cover about $20; bottle service from several hundred USD
Age policy
18+ (21+ Fri–Sat)

7eight7

Santurce · $$ · San Juan

houseedmreggaetonhip hop

One of San Juan's biggest mainstream clubs, a large-capacity open-format room at 1420 Av. Ponce de León anchored in house and EDM with reggaeton and hip-hop mixed in.

Hours
Thu–Sat 10pm–5am (some Wed nights)
DJs & nights
  • Open-format resident DJs
  • EDM & house headline nights
  • Reggaeton & hip-hop sets
Dress code
Enforced: dress shoes/button-ups or dresses/nice jeans; no athletic wear, shorts or flip-flops
Entry
Cover at the door; free entry possible via guest list on some nights
Prices
Cover typically $10–$20; cocktails around $10–$14
Age policy
18+

Fifty Eight

Condado · $$$ · San Juan

houseelectroniclatintop 40

Upscale resort nightclub at La Concha Resort on Ashford Avenue, with a dance floor that opens toward the beach and table service for groups.

Hours
Fri–Sat 10pm–4am (some Thu nights)
DJs & nights
  • Guest DJ nights
  • House & electronic sets
  • Special event parties
Dress code
Upscale and enforced: no shorts, flip-flops or athletic wear
Entry
Cover varies by event; table reservations recommended
Prices
Cover ranges from free to about $40 depending on the event
Age policy
18+

Kweens Klub

Santurce · $$ · San Juan

poplatindancehouse

The island's premier LGBTIQ+ nightclub: a two-floor drag and dance club open since 2021, with a resident cast of Puerto Rican queens and international guest stars.

Hours
Wed–Sun, from about 9pm to 3–4am
DJs & nights
  • Drag shows several nights a week
  • International guest queens
  • Queer Night DJ sets Fri–Sat
Dress code
Casual to party wear; costumes welcome on themed nights
Entry
Free cover Wed–Thu; cover applies Fri–Sat and for special events
Prices
Free midweek; Fri–Sat cover about $5–$10, more for headline events; drinks $6–$12
Age policy
18+

La Factoría

Old San Juan · $$ · San Juan

salsalatin jazzcocktail lounge

World-ranked multi-room cocktail bar on Calle San Sebastián — six bars in one, including a dedicated salsa dancing room; more bar than nightclub, but an essential San Juan night out.

Hours
Open daily from late afternoon until late (around 2am)
DJs & nights
  • Live salsa & Latin jazz nights
  • Multi-room DJ sets
  • Sunday salsa sessions
Dress code
Casual smart; come as you are
Entry
No cover; walk-ins only, expect a queue on weekends
Prices
No cover; cocktails about $10–$20
Age policy
23+
