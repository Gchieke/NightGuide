Best nightclubs in Mexico City

The 10 clubs

Looloo

Juárez · $$$ · Mexico City

housetechnodeep house

Mexico City's benchmark deep house and techno club on Londres 195, built around a retractable glass dome, industrial-loft design and a sound system that has hosted over 125 international artists.

Hours
Thu–Sat 10pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Danny Tenaglia
  • Solomun
  • Chris Avantgarde
  • Volkoder
Dress code
Smart and stylish; no caps or sportswear at the door
Entry
Advance tickets via Ticket Fairy and RA in priced phases that sell out; table reservations available
Prices
$290–$1,100 MXN depending on headliner and ticket phase
Age policy
18+

Janis Palmas

Lomas de Chapultepec · $$$ · Mexico City

housereggaetonpop

Upscale Janis outpost on Paseo de las Palmas, a high-energy, reservation-led nightclub with a packed dance floor, strong sound and visuals and a dressed-up Lomas crowd.

Hours
Wed–Sat 11pm–4am
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs leading the night
  • International guest DJs and live acts
  • Weekend bottle-service party nights
Dress code
Smart elegant; no shorts, sneakers or sportswear
Entry
Selective door; reservations and table booking strongly recommended
Prices
$500–$800 MXN per person
Age policy
18+

Sens

Bosques de las Lomas · $$$ · Mexico City

electronicreggaetontop 40

Bottle-service luxury club in Bosques de las Lomas where Mexico City's young upper class goes to be seen, with tables, guest DJs on weekends and a room that only fills after 1:30am.

Hours
Thu–Sat 11:30pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Resident DJs
  • Guest DJs on weekends
  • Bottle-service table nights
Dress code
Strictly enforced — button-down or polo and proper shoes for men, dresses and heels for women; no shorts, flip-flops or athletic sneakers
Entry
Cover at the door without a table; most weekends sell out by Thursday, so reserve tables via Instagram or WhatsApp in advance
Prices
$300–$500 MXN cover; small tables $4,000–$6,000 MXN minimum
Age policy
18+

Terraza Catedral

Centro Histórico · $ · Mexico City

housefunkdisco

Rooftop bar and club terrace above the Mundo Joven hostel with direct views of the Metropolitan Cathedral and the Zócalo, Mexican food, cocktails and weekly guest DJs.

Hours
Mon–Thu & Sun 1pm–11pm; Fri–Sat 2pm–12am
DJs & nights
  • Weekly guest DJs
  • Gorge
  • Timid Boy
Dress code
Casual; smart casual encouraged
Entry
Walk-ins welcome and no cover on most nights; reservations recommended at weekends and for sunset
Prices
Free entry most nights; moderate drink prices
Age policy
18+

technobreakbeatexperimental

Fri–Sat 11pm–5am
DJs & nights
  • Daniel Avery
  • Marie Davidson

  • HAAi

housetechnodisco

Iconic underground club hidden behind a former ice-cream parlour facade in the historic house of M. N. Roy at Mérida 186, with a double-height wooden pyramid over the dance floor and black basalt walls.

Hours
Thu–Sat 11pm–5:30am
DJs & nights
  • Johnny Chief
  • TANSEN
  • Discos Tabu takeovers
  • Mirá Mirá
Dress code
Dress to impress; selective access at the door
Entry
Former private members' club that still keeps selective access — reservation or guest list strongly recommended
Prices
$400–$900 MXN per person; entry around $500 MXN reported
Age policy
18+

Patrick Miller

Roma Norte · $ · Mexico City

hi-nrgitalo discodisco

Legendary Friday-only institution at Mérida 17 devoted to Hi-NRG and Italo disco since the 1980s, famous for its dance battles, themed retro nights and devoted 'Patricios' crowd.

Hours
Fri 9:30pm–2:30am
DJs & nights
  • Patrick Miller (Roberto Devesa)
  • Virginia Katana Castañeda
  • Hi-NRG Fridays
  • 80s and 90s retro nights
Dress code
Casual; comfortable clothes and shoes for dancing, no dress code
Entry
Walk-in only with a cheap cash cover at the door; drinks are bought with tokens inside and the room packs out by midnight
Prices
$100 MXN cover
Age policy
18+

electronichyperpophip-hop

Casual; no dress code
Entry

Age policy
18+

technohouseprogressive

Thu–Sat 11pm–5am
DJs & nights

$300–$500 MXN; typical entry around $500 MXN
