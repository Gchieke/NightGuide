technohouseedm

Four-floor, 25,000 sq ft Art Deco superclub inside the former Los Angeles Stock Exchange building, with a Funktion-One system and some of the biggest techno and house bookings in California.

Fri–Sat 10pm–3am
DJs & nights
  • Awakening techno nights (Insomniac)

Fashionable nightlife attire; no athletic wear, jerseys, tank tops, shorts or sandals
Entry
Advance tickets strongly recommended; tiered prices rise at the door, VIP tables available

$25–$60 entry
Age policy
