The Cove Barbados

St. Lawrence Gap · $$ · Bridgetown

socadancehallreggaehip hop

The Gap's main full-scale nightclub — the venue formerly known as the Reggae Lounge, rebuilt as a multi-room club with several dance floors and bars. A cornerstone of south-coast nightlife for locals and tourists alike.

Hours
Daily 8:00 PM–3:00 AM; Thu–Sat until 4:00 AM
DJs & nights
  • Free-entry Fridays (before 11 PM)
  • Thursday drink-special nights
  • Resident DJs across multiple rooms
Dress code
Smart casual
Entry
Free entry before 11 PM on Fridays; cover varies on other nights
Prices
Cover varies by night; free before 11 PM on Fridays
Age policy
18+

Harbour Lights

Bay Street, Carlisle Bay · $$ · Bridgetown

socadancehallcalypsopop

Barbados' most famous beach nightclub — an open-air venue on the sand at Carlisle Bay. Friday is club night with DJs and drinks-inclusive entry; Wednesdays and Thursdays host the long-running Beach Extravaganza dinner show.

Hours
Fri club night 9:00 PM–2:00 AM; dinner shows Wed & Thu 7:00 PM–10:30 PM
DJs & nights
  • Friday nightclub sessions with local DJs
  • Beach Extravaganza dinner show (Wed & Thu)
  • Live bands and cultural performances
Dress code
Casual beach-club attire
Entry
Cover charge at the door on club nights; dinner show is ticketed in advance
Prices
Friday club cover BBD $70 (US$35), including house drinks until 2:00 AM
Age policy
18+ (original photo ID required for the nightclub)
