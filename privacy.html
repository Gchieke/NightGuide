Privacy Policy — NightGuide

Last updated: October 18, 2026

NightGuide ("we", "our") is a worldwide nightclub discovery guide. Browse clubs city by city — hours, DJ lineups, dress codes, entry rules, and prices. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

NightGuide v1 does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline from its bundled club guide after the first load.

Club listings

Club details (names, hours, lineups, dress codes, prices) are curated from public sources. They are provided for general information only — always verify with the venue before heading out.

Changes

If future versions add accounts, favorites, or reviews, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the NightGuide repository.
